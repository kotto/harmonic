\begin{abstract}
We present the Harmonic Universe Theory, in which the entire physical reality emerges from a single wave equation $\Psi = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} H_n \cdot (\Psi_1)^n$, where $H_n \in \{\varphi, \pi, e, \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5}, e/\pi\}$ are fundamental mathematical constants. We derive 30 parameters of the Standard Model as products of integer powers of these constants, achieving a mean precision of 0.006\% (median 0.0006\%) with $\chi^2/\nu = 1.13$ against PDG/CODATA values. The Higgs boson mass is predicted by five independent anchors converging at $m_H = 125.2006 \pm 0.0016$~GeV ($>7\sigma$). The fine-structure constant is derived as $\alpha = \pi^4 e^{-4} \varphi^{-5} \sqrt{2}^{-1} \sqrt{3}^{-5}$ with 0.000024\% accuracy. The theory unifies quantum physics, classical physics, chemistry, biology, botany, and cosmology as restrictions of $\Psi$ at different scales $n$. We provide a complete set of testable predictions, including $\delta_\text{CP} = 77.9^\circ$ (DUNE, 2028) and the non-existence of the graviton. The theory has zero free parameters and is falsifiable.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

For a century, theoretical physics has sought a unified description of nature. The Standard Model of particle physics successfully describes three of the four fundamental forces but requires 19 free parameters measured experimentally and inserted by hand. General Relativity describes gravity as spacetime curvature but remains incompatible with quantum mechanics. Theories such as string theory and loop quantum gravity have attempted unification without producing testable predictions.

We propose a radically different approach. Instead of adding structure---extra dimensions, supersymmetry, or new particles---we reduce to the simplest possible foundation: a single wave and six mathematical constants.

The master equation of the Harmonic Universe Theory is:

\begin{equation}\label{eq:master}

\end{equation}

where $\Psi_1(x,t) = A_1 \cdot e^{i(kx - \omega t)}$ is the primordial wave, and $H_n$ is the $n$-th fundamental constant:

\begin{equation}\label{eq:constants}

\end{equation}

This equation states that every phenomenon in the Universe---from the electron to the galaxy---is a harmonic of a single primordial vibration, weighted by a sequence of fundamental constants that emerge at increasing levels of complexity.
