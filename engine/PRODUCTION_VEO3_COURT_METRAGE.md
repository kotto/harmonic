**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
Single continuous cinematic drone shot looking down over the Giza plateau at dawn, camera slowly descending from 200 feet towards the Great Pyramid of Khufu. The sun is just breaking the horizon behind the pyramid, creating a perfect golden silhouette with intense volumetric light rays and lens flare. The shadow of the pyramid stretches across the desert like a cosmic sundial. Sand texture extremely detailed, every grain visible. Dust particles float in the air, catching the golden light. Colors: deep blue sky fading into warm amber and ochre at the horizon. Film grain present, slight halation on highlights. The movement is slow, majestic, inexorable — like time itself moving. Aspect ratio: 2.39:1 anamorphic. 8K quality. No music, just the sound of wind and distant sand. The camera reaches the base of the pyramid, glides over the stones, each block 2.5 tons, perfectly fitted. The title card should appear at the very end of this shot: a single Greek letter "Ψ" in gold, followed by "L'ÉQUATION MÈRE" in a thin elegant serif font, centered on the pyramid face, then fading to black.

## SÉQUENCE 2 — LA MÉTHODE KA
**Durée cible : 40s | Type de plan : Intérieur, travelling circulaire, split-screen | Objectif : Montrer la méthode de déchiffrement et la découverte des constantes**

---

**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
Cinematic interior, researcher's study at night, warm amber light from a single desk lamp, the rest of the room in deep shadow. The protagonist (male, late 30s, intense eyes, slight beard, wearing a simple white shirt) sits at a desk covered in papyrus reproductions, spectral analysis charts, and phoneme tables. He wears large studio headphones and speaks into a microphone. Camera slowly orbits around him in a 180-degree arc.

Split screen composition: the left half of frame shows his face in profile, the right half of frame shows his computer screen. On the screen, an interface called "KA — Vocalisation Hiéroglyphique Assistée" displays ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs transforming into waveform patterns, then into numbers: π, φ, e appear one by one, glowing in gold.

The protagonist pronounces a word in reconstructed Ancient Egyptian — "Ka... Ka... Ka..." — and the waveform on screen resonates, splits into harmonics, and locks onto a perfect mathematical ratio. His eyes widen. He stops breathing. The light from the screen illuminates his face, blue and gold mixing.

The camera holds on his face for 5 seconds as realization dawns. Film grain, shallow depth of field, background out of focus — but we see the walls are covered with spectral analysis charts and Egyptian motifs. Volumetric light beams from the screen create dust motes in the air.

Color palette: deep blues (shadows), warm golds (screen light), amber (desk lamp). Aspect ratio: 2.39:1.
```

**🎬 PLAN B (si split-screen pose problème) :**
```
Traditional single-frame composition: protagonist in foreground typing at computer, screen in background left showing hieroglyph-to-waveform transformation, glass wall in background right covered with spectral charts. Camera slowly pushes in on his face as he makes the discovery. Simpler but equally effective.
```

Subterranean chamber deep inside the Great Pyramid of Giza, the so-called "cenotaph" in the bedrock. ## SÉQUENCE 4 — LE TEMPLE D'AKHENATON
**Durée cible : 30s | Type de plan : Grand-angle, contre-plongée, lumière divine | Objectif : L'intuition de l'onde primordiale**

---

**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
Open-air ruins of the Aten temple at Tell el-Amarna, Egypt. Midday. The temple has no roof — only foundations, altars, and the sky. The protagonist stands at the center of what was once the sanctuary, arms slightly away from his body, eyes closed, face tilted up to the sun.

Sunlight pours through a gap in the ruins, creating a single intense volumetric light beam that strikes the stone altar in front of him. The light is almost solid — we can see the dust motes swirling inside it. His shadow stretches long behind him.

The camera is positioned low, wide-angle lens, looking up at him against the sky. The ruins stretch to the horizon behind him, empty desert beyond. The light creates a lens flare with a golden hue.

As he stands there, the light beam begins to shimmer and transform: mathematical symbols appear inside the beam, floating, made of pure light. The equation "Ψ₁ = A₁·e^{i(ω₀t + φ₁)}" forms in the air in golden photons, each character glowing, rotating slowly.

He opens his eyes. He sees the equation. He whispers: "Avant toute chose... la lumière."

The camera holds this moment — man, light, equation — for 3 seconds, then the equation dissolves into the stone altar as if it had always been there.

Color palette: blinding gold, warm stone, deep blue sky. Volumetric light, intense sun flares, film halation. Aspect ratio: 2.39:1. The most beautiful shot of the film.
```

**🎬 PLAN B (si le métaphorique est trop complexe) :**
```
Same location, same protagonist, same light beam — but without the floating equation. Instead, the camera slowly zooms into his eye, and in the reflection of his eye we see the equation forming inside the pupil. More subtle, equally powerful.

## SÉQUENCE 6 — LE CHOC
**Durée cible : 45s | Type de plan : Push-in extrêmement lent, plan-séquence | Objectif : La dévastation, le silence — le cœur du film**

---

**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
A single unbroken shot. 45 seconds. The protagonist sits at his computer in near-darkness. The only light source is the monitor, illuminating his face from below. On the screen, two numbers:

0.707
0/935

The camera is at eye level, starting in a medium shot (head and shoulders). Every 5 seconds, the camera pushes in one millimeter — an imperceptible movement that accumulates. After 45 seconds, we are in extreme close-up: just his eyes, the screen reflection visible in his pupils.

During the first 20 seconds, he is frozen. He doesn't blink. He stares at the numbers.

At 20 seconds, he slowly brings his hand to his mouth. Still no blink.

At 30 seconds, a single tear forms at the corner of his right eye. It doesn't fall yet.

At 35 seconds, the tear falls.

At 40 seconds, he closes his eyes. The screen reflection disappears from his pupils.

At 45 seconds, black.

There is NO music. NO sound effects. Only: computer fan (constant, low), his breathing (shallow at first, then a long exhale at 30s), the tear hitting the desk (a single microscopic tap at 35s).

The lighting is cold, blue-white from the monitor, with deep black shadows. The grain is present but unobtrusive. Aspect ratio: 2.39:1.

This is the longest and most important shot of the film. It must land perfectly.
```

**🎬 PLAN B (si 45s ininterrompues impossible) :**

Split into three shots of 15 seconds each:
(1) Medium shot: protagonist frozen at screen, numbers visible
(2) Close-up: his face, the tear forming, the reflection in his eyes
(3) Extreme close-up: his eyes closing, fading to black

## SÉQUENCE 7 — LA NUIT DU FILTRE
**Durée cible : 40s | Type de plan : Orbite autour du tableau, ralenti | Objectif : La renaissance, la formulation des axiomes**

---

**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
The protagonist's study, midnight. The room is in chaos — papers everywhere, coffee cups, two weeks of sleepless nights. In the center stands a large whiteboard. The protagonist writes on it, erases, writes again. Camera orbits him slowly, at chest height, completing a full 360-degree rotation over 40 seconds.

On the whiteboard, four questions appear one by one in large handwriting:

1. QU'EST-CE QUI SURVIT AU TEMPS ?
2. QU'EST-CE QUI SURVIT À LA FORME ?
3. QU'EST-CE QUI SURVIT À LA STABILITÉ ?
4. QU'EST-CE QUI SURVIT À L'ÉLIMINATION ?

Each question stays on the board as the next is written. Under each, sub-equations form — the ABC kernel, the Mittag-Leffler function, the three stability conditions.

At 30 seconds, the protagonist stops. He writes in the center, larger than everything else, in letters that seem to glow:

"LA NATURE NE CHOISIT PAS : ELLE ÉLIMINE."

He steps back. The camera stops orbiting. Front-on. He stares at the sentence. The room is silent. A candle flickers on his desk.

He reads it aloud: "La nature ne choisit pas... elle élimine."

The camera holds on him for 10 seconds.

Lighting: single candle on the desk, one lamp in the corner. Deep shadows. His face half-lit, half-dark. The whiteboard catches the lamplight. Colors: warm amber, deep indigo. Film grain. 2.39:1.
```

**🎬 PLAN B :**
```
No continuous orbit. Three static positions: left profile (writing questions 1-2), front (writing questions 3-4 and the final sentence), right profile (reading aloud, reaction). Each 12 seconds.

## SÉQUENCE 8 — LA DÉRIVÉE D'OR
**Durée cible : 40s | Type de plan : Push-in + lumière ascendante | Objectif : La catharsis, la découverte de α = 1/φ**

---

**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
The study, 4 AM. The protagonist is at his computer, surrounded by printed papers — the Hurwitz theorem (1891), Mittag-Leffler functions, fractional calculus textbooks. He is pacing, then stops.

He walks to the whiteboard. He picks up the marker. He writes:

α = 1/φ

He stares at it. He writes:

cₙ = 1/Γ(n/φ + 1)

He runs to the keyboard. Types. The computer runs the FFT. The screen shows: 2.22 × 10⁻¹⁶.

The protagonist's hand hovers over the mouse. He doesn't need to run again. He knows.

The camera pushes in slowly on his face. First, his expression is blank — he doesn't know how to process it. Then, his lips part slightly. Then, his eyes water. Then, a smile — fragile, exhausted, real — breaks through.

Outside the window, the first light of dawn appears.

The camera tilts up from his face to the window. The sun rises over the city. The glass wall of the study is covered in equations, and the sunrise illuminates them from behind. The equations seem to catch fire with golden light.

The camera tilts back to his face. He is crying. He is smiling. He whispers: "La mémoire d'or."

Lighting: the transition from dark blue (4 AM) to warm golden (dawn). The equation on the computer screen glows. His face, first lit by the monitor, then by the rising sun. A single tear catches the golden light.

Aspect ratio: 2.39:1. Film grain, visible but subtle. Anamorphic. Emotional.
```

**🎬 PLAN B :**
```
Split into two shots: (1) protagonist writing α = 1/φ, reaction close-up, (2) sunrise through the glass wall, equations catching light, then cut back to his smiling/crying face. Shorter but preserves the emotional beats.
```

## SÉQUENCE 10 — LA QUESTION D'EINSTEIN
**Durée cible : 30s | Type de plan : Champ-contrechamp intime | Objectif : La réponse déterministe au probabilisme quantique**

---

**PROMPT VEO 3 :**
```
The protagonist's study, early dawn. The room is lit by the soft grey light before sunrise. He sits at his desk, exhausted but at peace. In his hands, a framed photograph of Albert Einstein at a blackboard, chalk in hand, the iconic 1953 portrait.

He speaks to the photograph:

"Tu as demandé, Albert, si Dieu joue aux dés..."

He pauses. A long pause. His thumb traces the edge of the frame.

"La réponse, je crois, c'est : non. Mais pas comme tu le pensais. Dieu ne joue pas aux dés parce qu'il n'a pas besoin de jouer. Les dés sont pipés par l'élimination."

He places the photograph on the desk, props it against a stack of books. He looks at Einstein, then at the equation on the glass wall behind.

"En dessous de tout, il y a l'onde. Et l'onde ne tire pas au hasard. Elle vibre, elle se décompose, elle filtre ce qui ne tient pas. Et ce qui tient... c'est nous."

The camera is on his face. Close. Intimate. The first true sunrise light enters the room and catches his face. He smiles — not triumphant, just... at peace.

Camera rack focus from his face to the photograph of Einstein. Then rack focus again to the equation on the glass wall in the background.

The moment holds. Silence. Then a single piano note.

Aspect ratio: 2.39:1. Warm, soft, intimate. The most human moment of the film.
```

**🎬 PLAN B :**
```
Single shot: protagonist sitting at desk, three-quarter profile, photograph in hand, addressing it. Camera holds at medium close-up throughout. The speech is delivered in one take. The light changes from grey dawn to golden sunrise during the shot (time-lapse effect).
