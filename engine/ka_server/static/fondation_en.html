Mission

An infrastructure problem, not a resources problem

Care exists. What is missing is circulation: records don't follow patients, payments don't cross borders, specialists are far away. KA Foundation builds the invisible infrastructure that connects what already exists.

📱

The record, not the hospital

History, allergies, prescriptions: a QR code in the patient's hands, readable by any caregiver in the ecosystem. The record belongs to the patient, not to the institution.

🌍

The diaspora as medical bridge

The family doctor lives in Paris or Brussels? The patient sends a secure teleconsultation link (30 minutes), and a cross-border second opinion becomes possible.

💚

Pay in UM, not in cash

A local unit, issued with the telecom operator, non-convertible for the patient (they cannot exchange it for currency), convertible for the caregiver. Money stays in the care loop.

The journey

From the village to the pharmacy, in five steps

Consultation

The doctor examines and writes the prescription in their app — it becomes a QR code.

Record

The prescription is added to the patient's portable record, accessible anywhere, to any authorized caregiver.

Pharmacy

The pharmacist scans the QR, verifies authenticity, dispenses. Payment in UM, instant.

Diaspora

Need a second opinion? The patient shares a teleconsultation link with their doctor abroad.

Laboratory

Results come back into the same record. The loop is closed, traceable, no lost paper.
