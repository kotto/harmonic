VITAL KA

The Sankofa Principle — it is not charity, it is return

Sankofa teaches us that we must go back to our roots to move forward. Supporting African healthcare is not charity — it is completing a circle that was broken.

Health disparities connect us

  • African Americans are 60% more likely to be diagnosed with diabetes
  • Hypertension affects 42% of Black Americans vs 28% of white Americans
  • The same diseases affecting your family in Chicago are affecting your family in Abidjan
  • Healthcare solutions developed for Africa will benefit African Americans too

Beyond remittances

  • Remittances are individual. Vital Ka enables collective impact.
  • A church in Atlanta can sponsor an entire clinic in Ghana
  • A sorority chapter can fund prenatal care for 100 women in Senegal
  • You see every patient treated. You know where every dollar went.
