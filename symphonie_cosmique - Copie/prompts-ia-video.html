SCENE 3 — Egypte antique : La Chambre de Khufu

INT. CHAMBRE DU ROI, GRANDE PYRAMIDE — AUBE · 12-15s

Epic reconstruction of ancient Egypt, 2500 BC. Interior of the King's Chamber inside the Great Pyramid of Giza at dawn. Walls of polished pink granite. Quartz crystals embedded in the walls shimmer with natural light. A shaft of sunlight enters through the northern air shaft. The architect IMHOTEP (African, visionary, simple linen garment, holding a measuring rod) traces geometric proportions on the granite floor — golden rectangles, circles, pentagons. Each traced line glows with golden light revealing mathematical constants: phi, pi, e, sqrt2, sqrt3, sqrt5, e/pi. Camera slowly pans across the walls covered in hieroglyphs. Each hieroglyph pulses gently and transforms into its corresponding modern mathematical symbol. The sarcophagus stands in the corner — its proportions encode e/pi. NOT a tomb — a resonator. A mathematical library in stone. Cinematic lighting. Mystical but realistic. Style reference: K2019 documentary by Femi Krasniqi.

In the bathtub facing him — his EXACT DOUBLE. Same face, same body, same position. Identical. The water is perfectly still. The man looks at his double with a mix of fear and awe. Mystical realism. No horror — wonder and mystery.

SCENE 9 — La Revelation (Scene cle du film)

INT. BUREAU — NUIT D'ORAGE · 15-20s

THE KEY SCENE. Small office in Yaounde during a tropical thunderstorm. Heavy rain beating against windows. Lightning flashes intermittently, illuminating the room. The African mathematician (45, exhausted, alone) stands before a whiteboard. On the left: Oyibo's equations. On the right: Atangana's equations. In a column: the 7 constants from Egyptian hieroglyphs. Photos of the Great Pyramid pinned around. A question mark in the center. His assistant sleeps on a chair in the corner. A massive lightning bolt illuminates everything — in that flash, the mathematician SEES something. He grabs a red marker and circles 1/phi on Oyibo's side, circles 0.618 on Atangana's side, circles phi in the hieroglyph column, and draws lines connecting all three. He writes the master equation. The equation GLOWS with intense golden light. The symbols LIFT OFF the board. Brief visions appear: the Great Pyramid, Fourier at his desk, Oyibo at his blackboard, Atangana in his lab, young Alain discovering the constants — all connected by a golden thread across time and space. The mathematician is in tears. The rain stops. Moonlight breaks through clouds. This must be TRANSCENDENT. The most important scene.
